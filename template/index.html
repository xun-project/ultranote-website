From Infinity to Ecosystem

XUNI is our history. UES is our direction.

A transparent transition

UltraNote Infinity (XUNI) is the legacy project. UES is a proposed new network in research and development. No UES mainnet, live UES wallet, token sale, migration portal or deposit address is offered on this website. Older XUNI software, links and specifications should not be treated as active UES services. Official updates will be published only after the relevant technical and release decisions are complete.
